Delivery Information

Shipping & Delivery

Everything you need to know about how your order gets to you.

Free delivery on all orders over ₦70,000. Standard delivery fee of ₦4,500 applies to orders below this amount within Lagos.

How We Process Your Order

Once we receive your order and confirm payment, we begin preparing your piece. For standard-size orders, this typically takes 1–2 business days. For custom measurement orders, production time varies — we'll confirm the timeline with you directly after receiving your measurements.

You will be contacted via WhatsApp or email when your order is dispatched, with tracking information where available.

Delivery Locations

We currently deliver across Nigeria. Delivery timelines and fees vary by state.

  • Lagos: Standard delivery 3–7 business days
  • Abuja: Standard delivery 5–8 business days
  • Other states: 5–10 business days — contact us to confirm availability and cost

If you are unsure whether we deliver to your location, please contact us before placing your order and we'll confirm.

Delivery Fees

  • Orders over ₦70,000 — Free delivery
  • Orders under ₦70,000 (Lagos) — ₦4,500
  • Orders outside Lagos — fee confirmed at time of order

Delivery fees are shown at checkout and confirmed when your order is received.

Custom Order Delivery

Custom measurement orders require additional production time beyond the standard processing period. Production timelines depend on the garment type and our current order schedule.

We will always communicate your estimated completion date after receiving your measurements. We ask for your patience — your piece is being made carefully, specifically for you.

Custom orders cannot be rushed once production has begun. If you have a specific event date, please mention it when placing your order so we can do our best to accommodate you.

Order Tracking

We will send you updates on your order via WhatsApp or email at dispatch. If you have not received an update within your expected delivery window, please contact us and we will investigate promptly.

Delivery Issues

If your order arrives damaged or there is an issue with your delivery, please contact us within 48 hours of receipt. Include your order details and photos of the issue, and we will resolve it as quickly as possible.
