Our Policy

Returns & Exchanges

Please read our policy carefully. Standard and custom orders are treated differently.

Standard Size Orders

Returns and exchanges may be accepted within the return window. Items must be unworn, unwashed and with all tags attached.

Custom Measurement Orders

Custom orders are made specifically to your measurements. They are not eligible for return or exchange unless there is a confirmed production fault.

Standard Size — Returns

If you are not satisfied with a standard-size purchase, you may request a return within [X] days of receiving your order, subject to the following conditions:

  • The item must be unworn, unwashed and in its original condition.
  • All original tags must be attached.
  • The item must be returned in its original packaging where possible.
  • Items showing signs of wear, staining, alteration or damage will not be accepted.
  • Sale items are final sale and cannot be returned.

To initiate a return, please contact us within the return window via WhatsApp or email with your order details and reason for return. We will provide return instructions upon approval.

Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer unless the item was received faulty or incorrectly.

Standard Size — Exchanges

We are happy to exchange a standard-size item for a different size, subject to availability. Please contact us within [X] days of receiving your order.

  • The item must meet the same return conditions listed above.
  • Exchanges are subject to availability of the requested size.
  • If the requested size is unavailable, a store credit or refund will be offered.

Custom Measurement Orders — Policy

Custom measurement orders are made specifically and entirely to your provided measurements. Because each piece is created uniquely for you, custom orders cannot be returned or exchanged unless:

  • There is a confirmed production fault (a defect in the garment itself).
  • The garment does not match the specifications confirmed with you during the order process.

We strongly recommend following our Measurement Guide carefully and contacting us if you are unsure about any measurement before your order is placed.

If you believe there is a fault with your custom order, please contact us within 48 hours of receipt with photos of the issue. We will review and respond promptly.

Faulty or Incorrect Items

If you receive an item that is faulty or not what you ordered, please contact us within 48 hours of receipt. Include:

  • Your name and order details
  • A description of the issue
  • Clear photographs of the fault or incorrect item

We will resolve all genuine faults as quickly as possible, either through a replacement, repair or refund as appropriate.

Refunds

Approved refunds will be processed within [X] business days of us receiving and inspecting the returned item. Refunds are issued via the original payment method or by bank transfer as agreed.

We do not offer refunds on custom measurement orders except in cases of confirmed production faults.

We're here to help. Please reach out and we'll do our best to resolve your concern.
