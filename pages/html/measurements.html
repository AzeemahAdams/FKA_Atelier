Your Perfect Fit

Measurement Guide

Because every body is unique. Here's how to measure yourself for the perfect FKA Atelier fit.

At FKA Atelier, we accept both standard sizes (XS–XXL) and custom measurements. Simply take your measurements using our guide below, and include them when placing your order. We'll handle the rest.

Step by Step

How to Take Your Measurements

Use a flexible tape measure. Measure over your undergarments or close-fitting clothing. Stand naturally — do not hold your breath or pull the tape too tight.

Bust

The fullest part of your chest.

1Wear a well-fitting bra.
2Wrap the tape around the fullest part of your bust.
3Keep the tape parallel to the floor.
4Note the measurement in centimetres.
Waist

Your natural waistline — the narrowest part of your torso.

1Find your natural waist by bending to one side — the crease is your waistline.
2Wrap the tape around this point.
3Keep one finger between tape and body for ease.
Hips

The fullest part of your hips and seat.

1Stand with feet together.
2Measure around the fullest part of your hips — usually 20–23cm below your waist.
3Keep the tape parallel to the floor.
Shoulder Width

Across the back from shoulder point to shoulder point.

1Ask someone to help with this measurement.
2Measure from the edge of one shoulder across the back to the other shoulder edge.
3Measure in a straight horizontal line.
Sleeve Length

From shoulder point to wrist.

1Slightly bend your elbow.
2Measure from the edge of your shoulder, over the elbow, to your wrist bone.
3This gives a comfortable sleeve length.
Full Length

From shoulder to desired hem.

1Stand upright and relaxed.
2Measure from the top of your shoulder straight down to where you'd like the garment to end.
3Specify: full length, midi or knee length.
