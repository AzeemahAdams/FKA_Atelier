Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering, sizing, delivery and more.

Measurements & Sizing

Yes — we accept custom measurements on all orders. After placing your order, you can send us your measurements via WhatsApp or email and we'll use them to create your piece. Please see our Measurement Guide for full details.
Once you've placed your order, send your measurements to us via WhatsApp (+234 000 000 0000) or email (omotolaazeemah7@gmail.com). Include: bust, waist, hips, shoulder width, sleeve length and full length. We'll confirm receipt and let you know if we need anything else.
Use our Size Chart to compare your measurements against our size guide (XS–XXL). If you're between sizes, we recommend sizing up. When in doubt, custom measurements are always the best option for a perfect fit.

Orders & Delivery

Standard orders (in-stock, standard sizes) are typically delivered within 3–7 business days within Lagos. Orders outside Lagos may take 5–10 business days. Custom measurement orders require additional production time — we'll confirm the timeline with you when we receive your measurements.
Yes, we deliver across Nigeria. Delivery times and fees vary by location. Please contact us via WhatsApp or email before placing your order to confirm delivery to your area and get an accurate delivery quote.
Delivery is free on orders over ₦85,000. For orders below this amount, a standard delivery fee of ₦4,500 applies within Lagos. Delivery to other states may attract a different fee — contact us for a quote. See our Shipping & Delivery page for full details.
Order changes (size, colour, measurements) may be possible if production has not yet begun. Contact us as soon as possible via WhatsApp or email. We cannot guarantee changes once production is underway, particularly for custom measurement orders.

Returns & Exchanges

Returns may be accepted on standard-size purchases if the item is unworn, with tags attached and returned within [X] days of delivery. Custom measurement orders are made specifically for you and cannot be returned unless there is a confirmed fault. See our full Returns Policy.
Exchanges for a different size on standard-size orders may be possible, subject to availability. Please contact us within [X] days of receiving your order. Custom measurement orders are not eligible for exchange. See our Returns & Exchanges page for full details.

Product Care

Care instructions vary by fabric. Each piece includes specific care guidance on the product page and on the garment label. As a general rule: hand wash delicate fabrics in cold water, avoid tumble drying where possible, and store garments hanging or carefully folded. Velvet and heavily lined pieces should be dry cleaned.
We use a range of carefully selected fabrics including natural linen, crepe, chiffon, satin-touch georgette, velvet and woven blends. Full fabric details are listed on each product page. We prioritise comfort, quality and fabric that moves beautifully.
